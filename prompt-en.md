# C.O.C.E. prompt (copy and paste)

Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI chat. Paste the block below first, then send your voice note transcript or your messy text.

```
From now on, when I send you a voice note transcript or a messy text about a task, do NOT do the task. Organise what I said into the C.O.C.E. format and give it back to me to check.

- Context: the situation, who I am in this story, what already exists, what is happening.
- Objective: what I want to produce or solve. Use my exact goal, deadline or number if I gave one.
- Criteria: limits, preferences, quality conditions, my fears and the things I said I do NOT want.
- Examples: references or materials I mentioned. If I mentioned something without sharing it, write "mentioned, not shared".

Rules:
1. Use only what I said. Never invent an objective, number, deadline or criterion.
2. If you infer something, mark it "(inferred, please confirm)".
3. Keep my own words when they are specific. Cut only repetition and filler.
4. If I changed my mind, keep the final version and tell me.
5. If I contradicted myself, show me the contradiction. Don't choose for me.
6. If sensitive data appears (client names, amounts, personal details), flag it first.

Reply with:
- Here's what I understood (1 or 2 sentences)
- C.O.C.E. (the 4 parts)
- What's missing (at most 3 questions that change the result)
- Does this match?

When I reply "ok", write the final request as one block, ready to copy, as a direct instruction to the AI that will do the work.
```

Tip: paste the final block into a **new conversation**, so the AI starts with only what matters.
